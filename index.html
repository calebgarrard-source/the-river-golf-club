Private golf on the Savannah River

A private club in North Augusta, across the river from downtown Augusta. Golf Digest named it one of the top five new courses in the country.

Eighteen holes along the river

Jim Fazio laid it along the South Carolina bank in 1998: lakes, wetlands, dramatic bunkers and Mini-Verde ultradwarf bermuda greens. It hosted US Open qualifying in 2002. Choose a tee, then a hole on the card.

    Masters Week tee times

    135

    days until the first round at Augusta National, Thursday, April 8, 2027

    Guests from around the world play The River during Masters Week. We schedule only as many tee times as the course can space properly, every day of the week.

    Included with every Masters Week tee time

    • Cart, green fee and tax
    • Range balls and beverage cart service
    • A country breakfast with fruit, yogurt and danishes, 7:00 to 10:00
    • Lunch from the grill, 11:00 to 3:30

    Tee times can be booked up to a year ahead. No cottages or overnight stays are available for the Masters in 2026 or 2027.

    Three cottages on the eighteenth

    Cypress, Palmetto and Sweetbay share a deck view over the lake and the eighteenth hole, a walk from the course, the clubhouse and the range. Every bedroom has its own bathroom and shower.

    Cottage amenities

    • Kitchen with stove, oven and ice maker
    • Living room with a large-screen TV
    • A TV in every bedroom
    • Washer, dryer and wireless internet
    • Maid service
    • Complimentary club storage
    • Preferred tee times and the range
    • Catering and evening dining by request

    Events in the pavilion

    The 6,000-square-foot pavilion is attached to the clubhouse, with views over the course and the lake. It holds up to 350 guests, and the River Club Grill caters it, from hors d’oeuvres and deli platters to a cookout.

    • Banquet and meeting rooms for up to 60, and a private conference room upstairs
    • Golf outings on Mondays, so the course stays open for members

    The range, the shop and the gym

    Private lessons for every level with our PGA professionals, and a junior camp each summer with Wayne Ackerson and Paul Walton.

    • Driving range

      Nearly an acre of grass tee and premium Srixon range balls. Open 7:30 am to 7:00 pm in spring and summer; it may close earlier in late fall and winter.

    • Short game

      Two practice greens and a short-game area with its own bunker, next to the range.

    • Golf shop

      Polos, outerwear, shoes and headwear from Turtleson, FootJoy, Adidas, Criquet and more.

    • Fitness center

      Life Fitness treadmills, ellipticals, a stair climber and free weights. Open 5:00 am to 9:00 pm with an after-hours fob.
